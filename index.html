01  Findings

Four positions, stated plainly

Each one is written the way a position on a return is written: what it is, what it costs, and why it happened. No blame attaches to any of it.

Finding 01

The support address published for Credit Brilliance cannot receive mail.

Cost

Enquiries sent to it vanish without a bounce, so nobody ever knows to follow up.

Cause

The mailbox stayed on the brand's original domain after that domain lapsed.

Finding 02

The credit brand's audience is being sent to a domain that is now listed for sale.

Cost

The warmest audience that brand owns, built over years of reviews and video, lands on a holding page and leaves.

Cause

The link was set once, correctly, and nothing re-checks where published links actually land.

Finding 03

GoHighLevel is paid for and correctly founded, but it is wired to nothing.

Cost

Leads, bookings and follow up are still handled by hand, outside the system you already own.

Cause

Setup stopped at the foundation. That part takes an afternoon. The part that pays is everything built above it.

Finding 04

Nothing in the funnel is measured, at any point.

Cost

The strong application page you already have could be producing results you would never be able to see, or repeat.

Cause

The page carries no measurement, and the live campaign is built to start conversations rather than to create records.
